# Campaign Launch

A landing page and paid campaign for one offer. The brief must state the budget, dates and countries.

| # | Step | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete the brief | Lead with human | `brief.md` |
| | **Gate G1: Brief** | Human | |
| 2 | Offer, audience, angles | strategist | `deliverables/strategy.md` |
| 3 | Campaign structure, budget, test plan | paid-media | `deliverables/campaign-plan.md` |
| 4 | Landing page structure | ux-designer | `deliverables/ux.md` |
| | **Gate G2: Direction** | Human | |
| 5 | Landing page copy and ad variants | copywriter | `deliverables/copy/` |
| 6 | Conversion events and UTMs | analytics | `deliverables/tracking-plan.md` |
| 7 | Build page and tracking on preview | web-developer | Preview URL |
| 8 | Cross-review of SEO, tracking and copy fit, then fixes | seo-specialist, analytics, web-developer | Updated preview |
| 9 | Review page, ads and tracking | qa-reviewer | `deliverables/qa-report.md` |
| | **Gate G3: Launch** | Human | |
| 10 | Publish the landing page | web-developer | Live URL |
| 11 | Confirm live conversion events and lead delivery | analytics | Handoff to lead |
| | **Gate G4: Spend** | Human | |
| 12 | Activate campaigns as approved | Human, or lead with explicit approval | Live campaigns |
| 13 | First-week review | analytics, paid-media | `deliverables/reports/` |

At G4 the human sees the exact budget, dates, targeting and final ads. Later budget or targeting changes need a new G4 approval.
