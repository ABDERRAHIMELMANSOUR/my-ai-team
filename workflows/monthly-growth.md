# Monthly Growth

A recurring cycle for a live site and its campaigns. Save each month's work in `deliverables/reports/YYYY-MM/`.

| # | Step | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Provide or connect last month's data | Human or lead | Data for analytics |
| 2 | Performance report | analytics | `performance-report.md` |
| 3 | SEO review: rankings, pages, technical issues | seo-specialist | Prioritized fixes |
| 4 | Ads review: spend, waste, test results | paid-media | Recommended changes |
| 5 | Combine into one plan with at most five priorities | Lead with strategist | `plan.md` |
| | **Approve plan and any spend change (G4)** | Human | |
| 6 | Write content and ad variants | copywriter | `deliverables/copy/` |
| 7 | Site fixes and content on preview | web-developer | Preview URL |
| 8 | Cross-review of SEO, tracking and copy fit, then fixes | seo-specialist, analytics, web-developer | Updated preview |
| 9 | Review before publishing | qa-reviewer | `qa-report.md` |
| | **Gate G3: Launch** | Human | |
| 10 | Publish and record what changed | web-developer, lead | Entry in `decisions.md` |

Record what changed and when, so next month's report can connect results to specific actions. Skip any step with no work that month, and note the skip in `status.md`.
