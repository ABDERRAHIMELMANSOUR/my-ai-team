# Team workflow

## 1. Define the brief

Copy [project-brief-template.md](project-brief-template.md) to a project-specific brief. Identify audience, business goal, scope, constraints, success metrics, and decision maker.

## 2. Plan and assign

The [Team Lead](../agents/team-lead.md) breaks the brief into tasks. Each task records its owner, inputs, dependencies, deliverable path, and acceptance criteria. Track status as Backlog, Ready, In progress, Review, or Done.

## 3. Produce and coordinate

The designer and SEO specialist inform the developer's requirements. The content marketer aligns messaging with the design and search intent. The analytics specialist defines measurement before implementation or campaign launch. The paid ads specialist prepares campaign proposals using the brief, landing page, and tracking plan.

## 4. Review

Check each deliverable against the brief. Review website behavior, responsive layout, accessibility, search requirements, and measurement. Review marketing claims, audience fit, destinations, budget assumptions, and KPI definitions. Mark untested items explicitly.

## 5. Deliver or launch

Prepare a reviewable result and obtain authorization for deployment, external publication, or advertising spend. Record what was authorized and released. Do not mark a task Done until its acceptance criteria are satisfied or an exception is recorded.

## 6. Measure and improve

Record source, reporting dates, timezone, currency, attribution settings, and metric definitions. Compare results with the agreed baseline and target. Distinguish observed results from hypotheses.
