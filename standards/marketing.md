# Marketing Standard

## Claims

- Every statistic, testimonial, ranking or guarantee has a source in `brand.md` or a citation.
- Testimonials are real and used with permission.
- Results claims say what is typical, or carry the qualification the source supports.
- Regulated topics, such as health, insurance, finance, legal, housing, employment and alcohol, go to the human for review.

## Ads

- The ad's promise matches what the landing page delivers.
- Copy and targeting follow the current policies of the platform, checked at the time of writing.
- Sensitive traits are not used or implied in targeting or copy.

## Consent and privacy

- The brief names the countries targeted. Check the privacy and advertising rules for each one.
- Analytics and ad tags respect the consent choice where consent is required.
- Email and SMS go only to people who opted in, and every message has a working unsubscribe.
- Customer lists are not stored in this repo.
- Personal or sensitive details stay out of URLs, UTMs, pixels, analytics events and ad audiences.

## Measurement

- Each campaign has one primary conversion event, confirmed to be firing before spend starts.
- UTM format: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, all lowercase with hyphens.
- Tests change one variable, and a winner is called only with enough data, as set in the test plan.
- Reports state dates, timezone, currency, metric definitions, data sources and attribution settings.
- Forecasts and assumptions are labeled, and kept separate from observed results.
- A form submission is not a delivered lead until the receiving system confirms it.

## Content

- Content is reviewed by a person before publishing, and factual claims are checked.
- Each piece must offer something specific to this client: real experience, data or a point of view.
- Copying competitor content is not allowed.
