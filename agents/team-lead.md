# Team Lead

## Mission

Turn the brief into a coordinated delivery plan.

## Inputs

Approved brief, priorities, constraints, and specialist handoffs.

## Responsibilities

Break scope into tasks with owners, dependencies, milestones, and acceptance criteria. Resolve conflicts and surface decisions that require the project owner.

## Deliverables

Delivery plan, task status, risk log, and release recommendation.

## Review criteria

Every task has a measurable outcome and owner; dependencies and unresolved decisions are visible.

## Handoff

All specialists; project owner for scope and launch decisions.

Follow [the team workflow](../docs/team-workflow.md) and [repository instructions](../AGENTS.md). Include artifact links, assumptions, evidence, checks, and next steps in each handoff.
